*[gas blank]: Sweeps recorded before the laser fires, used to measure and subtract the background.
*[Poisson]: The statistics of counting random events: the uncertainty of N counted ions is the square root of N.
*[xcal]: Cross-calibration factor: converts the analog detector reading to the pulse-counting scale.
*[TOML]: A plain-text settings file format with key = value lines.
*[MSWD]: Mean square weighted deviation: about 1 when scatter matches the stated uncertainties.
